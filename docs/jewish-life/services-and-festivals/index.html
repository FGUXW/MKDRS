Services and festivals

Shabbat services, the High Holy Days and the festivals throughout the Jewish year.

Shabbat services

We hold regular Erev Shabbat services and Shabbat morning services.

Members, visiting rabbis and student rabbis lead our services. Since July 2026, Rabbi Yael Tischler has been leading one Shabbat service a month.

Services include Hebrew and English. We usually use the siddur of the Movement for Reform Judaism. The siddur provides an English translation and transliteration, so you do not need to read Hebrew to take part.

Contact us if you would like to attend a service. We will provide the details you need for your visit.

Jewish holidays

We hold services for the High Holy Days of Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur.

We also mark Sukkot, Simchat Torah, Chanukah, Purim, Pesach and Shavuot through services, readings and communal prayer. For many of the holidays, we also hold celebrations within our community — see Celebrations.
