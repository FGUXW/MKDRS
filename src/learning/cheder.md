Our Cheder gives children the chance to learn, make friends and feel at home in their Jewish community.

They learn to read and use Hebrew through songs, prayers, games and practical activities. Jewish studies helps them explore festivals, traditions, stories, values and what it means to be Jewish today.

We want children to enjoy being Jewish, build confidence and take an active part in Jewish life as they grow.

Children can join from age 5. Younger children are also welcome when accompanied by an adult.
