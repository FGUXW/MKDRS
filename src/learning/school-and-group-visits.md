A visit can introduce the synagogue, Jewish worship, festivals, religious objects and everyday Jewish life. We can adapt sessions for different ages and learning needs.

We are also happy to visit schools and provide a speaker for an assembly, workshop or professional learning session.

## Groups we have worked with

- Nurseries and children's centres
- Primary and secondary schools
- Special schools
- Scout groups
- Women's Institute groups
- Churches and faith groups
- Local religious education teachers
