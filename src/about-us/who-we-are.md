Our synagogue is based in Milton Keynes, with members joining us from across Buckinghamshire, Bedfordshire and Northamptonshire.

We are part of the Movement for Progressive Judaism and welcome people with different backgrounds, traditions and ways of being Jewish.

Since 1978, MKDRS has grown into a small, friendly community where people know one another, share celebrations, learn together, and offer support when it is needed.

Join us for services, festivals, learning and community activities.
