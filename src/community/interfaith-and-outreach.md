MKDRS works with local faith and community organisations to build understanding and good relationships across Milton Keynes.

We take part in Interfaith MK, the Council of Faiths and the Dialogue Society. Members also contribute to civic events, Holocaust remembrance and local educational work.

Our outreach includes synagogue visits, talks about Judaism and participation in interfaith events.
