Our choir has been a much-loved part of MKDRS since 1986.

It sings at in-person services, occasional concerts and leads services from time to time. The choir has also represented us at local interfaith and civic events, including:

- The Milton Keynes Holocaust Memorial Day service
- One Voice multicultural concerts
- The Big Iftar
- The Mayor of Milton Keynes carol service

Some members of the MKDRS choir regularly sing in the Abrahamic Women's Choir.
