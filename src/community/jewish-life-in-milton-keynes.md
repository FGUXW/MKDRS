Jewish life in Milton Keynes centres on MKDRS.

We are a small congregation, so people get to know each other. Members build relationships across different ages, backgrounds and levels of observance.

## Our community

- Our members come from Milton Keynes and nearby towns and villages across Buckinghamshire, Bedfordshire and Northamptonshire.
- We hold services, festival celebrations, Cheder sessions, social events and community activities.
- Members support each other through important moments in life.
- Milton Keynes has good rail and road connections, schools, major employers, parks, lakes, theatres, cinemas and sports facilities.

Some larger supermarkets stock kosher products. [Contact us](/contact-us/) if you are considering moving to the area and would like to speak to someone from the congregation.
