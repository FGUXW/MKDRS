title: Celebrations
intro: We celebrate the major Jewish festivals together throughout the year.
---
At Chanukah, we light the candles, sing familiar songs, eat traditional food and enjoy activities for children and adults.

Purim is one of our liveliest celebrations. We read the Megillah, dress up, make plenty of noise and share food and fun together.

At Pesach, we hold a community seder, where members of all ages gather around the table to retell the story of the Exodus, sing, ask questions and share the festival meal.

We also come together for festivals such as Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot and Shavuot. Some are marked through services, while others include meals, learning, music or activities for the whole community. The Cheder children often perform plays at festival celebrations.
