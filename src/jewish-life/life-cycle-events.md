MKDRS supports members as they mark important events in Jewish life and over the years our members have celebrated baby blessings, Bar or Bat Mitzvah ceremonies and weddings.

We normally ask people who are not members to join the community before arranging a life cycle event.

We can also provide current information about mohalim registered with the Association of Reform and Liberal Mohalim.
