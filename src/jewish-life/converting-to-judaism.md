We welcome people who are exploring conversion to Judaism.

MKDRS offers a warm and supportive community for people at different stages of the conversion process. You can attend services, meet members and learn more about Jewish life.

Conversion is a significant personal commitment. It includes learning, participation in community life and guidance from an appropriate rabbinic authority.

Use the conversion enquiry option in the [contact form](/contact-us/).
